braidedU

A startup for student braiders and their clients

The problem
At Cornell, finding a braider means digging through group chats and DMs. Prices are unclear, availability is a guess, and settling on a time can take days of back and forth. Braiders lose bookings in the shuffle, and clients lose hours chasing replies.
What I built
I built braidedU to put it all in one place. Clients browse braiders, compare styles, prices and durations upfront, and request a time that works. Braiders set their own hours and manage requests, payments and reviews on the same site.
Where it stands
Live and onboarding. Student braiders and early adopters are joining the platform.
Projected impact
My goal is one booking request in place of days of messaging. Year one projection: about 240 student clients, 720 bookings and 300 hours of messaging saved.

React, TypeScript, Django REST Framework, PostgreSQL

Gradebook Ghana

A free gradebook for Ghanaian high schools

The problem
Many Ghanaian high schools still record grades on paper. Teachers spend hours adding up scores and copying them onto report cards by hand, and students can wait months to see their results.
What I built
I built Gradebook Ghana as a free, web based gradebook. Schools set up classes, subjects and grading scales, teachers enter each assessment once, and the system does the math. Students log in to see their results and download their report cards, and admins, teachers and students each get their own view.
Where it stands
Live demo, built for Ghanaian schools.
Projected impact
My goal is report cards in days instead of months. Pilot projection: 10 schools and 8,000 students, saving about 3,200 teacher hours a year.

React, TypeScript, Spring Boot, PostgreSQL
