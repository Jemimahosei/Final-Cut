braidedU

Making it easier for Cornell students to find and book braiders.

The problem

Finding someone to braid your hair at Cornell can involve searching through group chats, scrolling through Instagram pages, and messaging different braiders just to compare prices or find an available appointment. I wanted to make that process easier for students while also giving student braiders a better way to manage their businesses.

What I built

I built braidedU, a booking platform where students can explore hairstyles, compare prices, and request appointments with student braiders. Braiders can manage their availability and booking requests through their own dashboard. I developed the frontend using React and TypeScript, built the backend with Django REST Framework, and used PostgreSQL to manage the application's data.

Where it stands

The platform is live, and I'm currently working on onboarding braiders and early users.

Projected impact

My goal is one booking request in place of days of messaging. Year one projection: about 240 student clients, 720 bookings and 300 hours of messaging saved.

React, TypeScript, Django REST Framework, PostgreSQL

Gradebook Ghana

Making grade management easier for schools in Ghana.

The problem

Growing up in Ghana, I was familiar with how much work goes into recording grades, calculating scores, and preparing student report cards. For schools still relying on manual processes, these tasks can take up valuable time that teachers could otherwise spend with students.

What I built

I built Gradebook Ghana, a free online gradebook designed to make that process easier. Teachers can enter assessment scores, calculate grades, and generate report cards, while students can access their results and administrators can manage classes and grading systems. I built the frontend using React and TypeScript, developed the backend with Spring Boot, and used PostgreSQL for data storage.

Where it stands

The application is available as a live demo. My goal is to eventually make it accessible to schools that could benefit from a simpler and more affordable way to manage student grades.

Projected impact

My goal is report cards in days instead of months. Pilot projection: 10 schools and 8,000 students, saving about 3,200 teacher hours a year.

React, TypeScript, Spring Boot, PostgreSQL

Research assistant, Interpersonal Perception and AI Mediation

Cornell University, InterPlay Research Studio, Fall 2026

I review selected interactions and help develop a coding framework that connects interpersonal rating patterns to observable conversational behaviors. I'm also exploring how language models and multimodal features can help identify these patterns more systematically.

Read moreRead less

I'm working on research exploring how AI can recognize and eventually help mediate conflict during group conversations. Our team studies how people's perceptions of one another change during two person and three person interactions. We use interpersonal rating data to identify patterns such as disagreement, deterioration, and recovery, then examine the corresponding video, audio, and transcripts to understand what happened during those moments. The longer term goal is to use this research to develop an AI mediator that can recognize emerging conflict and support more productive conversations.

About me

I'm an Information Science student at Cornell University, concentrating in Data Science and Artificial Intelligence, with a strong interest in software engineering.

I'm especially interested in full stack development, AI, and building technology that makes everyday tasks easier for the people using it.

What I enjoy most about programming is being able to build something from an idea. A lot of my projects started with problems I noticed in my own life or community, and I like figuring out how to turn those problems into software that could make things easier for someone else.

Growing up in Ghana and studying at Cornell have shaped the kinds of problems I care about. They've also given me opportunities to work with different people and see how technology can be useful in very different settings.

My research has made me more interested in the relationship between people and technology, especially how AI systems behave in real world interactions. I enjoy both the technical side of building systems and understanding how people will actually use them.

Outside my own projects, I'm involved in Women in Computing and NSBE at Cornell, and I volunteer with Girls Who Code.

When I'm not coding, I enjoy skiing, archery, dancing, and reading.
